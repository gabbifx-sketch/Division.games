So, what can you actually do here?

You can explore different browser games, search for specific titles, browse categories, and jump straight into a game. Division includes racing, driving, action, shooting, multiplayer, simulation, fighting and many other genres.

What devices does Division work on?

Division is made for the web, so it can be used on supported browsers across Chromebooks, laptops, desktops and other compatible devices. If your browser can run the game, you should be ready to play.

Why the name Division?

Division brings different games, genres and experiences together in one place. Instead of focusing on only one type of game, the site is built to give players different choices depending on what they feel like playing.

Does Division own all the games on the site?

No. Division does not own all of the games featured on the platform. Some games are embedded from other websites and game providers. Our job is to find games that are fun and interesting, organize them on Division, and provide a smooth way for players to discover and play them. We carefully choose games that we think are worth featuring and continue working to improve the experience around them.

Who's behind Division?

Division was created and developed by Borquaye Gabriel, a young developer from Ghana with an interest in coding, technology and gaming. The project is being built step by step, from the design and game pages to new features and improvements.

Is Division finished?

Not yet. Division is still growing. New games, features, improvements and changes will continue to be added as the project develops.
